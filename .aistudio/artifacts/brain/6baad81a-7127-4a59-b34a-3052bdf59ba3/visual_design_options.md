# Record Book — 3 Visual UI Design Concepts

Here are the 3 complete visual UI design mockups for **Record Book**:

---

## Option 1: Swiss Editorial FinTech (Mercury / Stripe / Wise Style)
![Option 1 - Swiss Editorial FinTech](https://ais-dev-afri75nyvwpdad3pa3k4zb-561167171247.asia-southeast1.run.app/assets/designs/option1.jpg)

- **Palette & Feel**: Crisp alabaster-white surface (`#F8FAFC`), deep forest-emerald primary accents (`#059669`), razor-sharp `1px` borders, and high-contrast typography.
- **Key Highlights**:
  - Minimalist light sidebar with a deep emerald active pill.
  - 4 top KPI cards with built-in micro-sparklines and mini bar charts (**Cash & Bank Liquidity**, **Total Inflow**, **Khata Receivables**, **Credit Card Bills**).
  - Center dual-curve **Cashflow Trend** chart (`7D / 30D / 90D`) paired with a **Khata Udhar Reminders** list featuring 1-tap **Send via WhatsApp** buttons.
  - Clean borderless **Recent Transactions** table at the bottom.

---

## Option 2: Warm Tactile Wealth Bento (Apple Wallet / Monzo / Crextio Style)
![Option 2 - Warm Tactile Wealth Bento](https://ais-dev-afri75nyvwpdad3pa3k4zb-561167171247.asia-southeast1.run.app/assets/designs/option2.jpg)

- **Palette & Feel**: Warm sand-alabaster canvas (`#F6F4EE`), deep charcoal-ebony floating sidebar (`#18181B`) with warm amber-gold active pills, and coral, sage-green, and ochre bento cards.
- **Key Highlights**:
  - Top welcome bar with sphere switcher pills (**All Spheres · Business · Personal · Family**).
  - Dark charcoal **Hero Treasury Card** with a circular **Solvency Gauge** paired with a 2×2 grid of tactile pastel KPI cards.
  - Bottom row featuring a realistic **Bank & Credit Card Stack** (showing Billed vs. Unbilled Dues), a **Khata Customer Receivables** checklist, and a **Weekly Velocity** bar chart.

---

## Option 3: Obsidian & Emerald Luxury Terminal (Linear / Revolut Ultra Style)
![Option 3 - Obsidian & Emerald Luxury Terminal](https://ais-dev-afri75nyvwpdad3pa3k4zb-561167171247.asia-southeast1.run.app/assets/designs/option3.jpg)

- **Palette & Feel**: Deep obsidian-carbon dark glass (`#090D16`), `1px` translucent borders, and glowing emerald, cyan, amber, and rose indicators.
- **Key Highlights**:
  - Top row of 4 glowing glassmorphic KPI cards with status dots and crisp monospace figures.
  - Full-width dual-wave **Cashflow** chart above a multi-segment **Capital Pipeline** allocation bar.
  - Bottom row with uniform-size **Encrypted Vault**, **Bank Accounts**, and **Credit Cards** cards alongside a **Real-Time Ledger** table.
